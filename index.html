What this is

Every day an automated harness opens a fixed list of public news and media sites in browsers with synthetic, automated profiles that persist across days (no personal browsing, no logins), and records which ad-delivery hosts the pages contact. The browsers and devices in these runs are: chromium; chromium / Pixel 5; chromium / iPhone 15; chromium / yandex-ad-sites; firefox; firefox / ETP off / yandex-ad-sites; firefox / iPhone 15; firefox / yandex-ad-sites. Each site is observed for 60 or 75 s with consent dialogs accepted where one is recognised, the page scrolled and one article opened. The extension only observes; it does not block or change traffic. Nothing on this page is about a person or a real device.

Reproduce a day

From a fresh checkout of the repository, one command repeats a published run and compares it with the published file:

pnpm observatory:reproduce -- --date=2026-11-05 --variant=chromium --from=<site URL or directory>

Auctions differ from one visit to the next, so the comparison is made on what is stable: the same site list and variant; sites loaded within 10 % of the published count (never stricter than one site); median third-party domains within 50 %. Documented hosts are listed as shared, only in the published run, or only in the new run, without a pass or fail on them.

Method and limitations

  • Contacts with documented infrastructure show exposure to the ad-delivery path described in public reporting; they do not show targeting or anything delivered to a device. Selective responses, validator or C2 contact and correlated chains would be the stronger signals.
  • Runs before 2026-11-03 used the pre-retune redirect_chain rule and an unreliable third-party counter; compare severities and counts from later runs only.
  • The severity columns leave out page.hidden_iframe_after_load: it fires on ordinary ad slots on most sites and is counted separately under "Hidden iframes". Any of those detections that is elevated or above still appears under "Signals that matter".
  • Absence of signals means the automated profiles were not shown the documented infrastructure in these auctions; it says nothing about other profiles or devices.
  • The profiles are synthetic and automated, the site list is fixed and small, and each site is visited once per variant and day. The numbers describe those visits only.
